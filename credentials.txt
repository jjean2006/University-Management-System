4. DYNAMIC USER CREATION (ADMIN DASHBOARD)
================================================================================
The Administrator dashboard at http://localhost:5000/admin/dashboard includes an
"Add Institutional User & Entity Account" card.

When an Admin adds a user:
  • Role "Student": Inserts full student attributes into the STUDENT table
    (Reg No, Names, Email, DOB, Gender, CGPA, Programme, Advisor, Room) and
    creates a synchronized login in USER_ACCOUNT.
  • Role "Faculty": Inserts full faculty attributes into the FACULTY table
    (Employee Code, Names, Email, Phone, Designation, Department) and creates a
    synchronized login in USER_ACCOUNT.
  • Role "Admin" / "Accounts" / "Warden": Creates an administrative staff login
    directly in USER_ACCOUNT.

Newly added users can immediately log in at http://localhost:5000/login using their
assigned username/code and password.
